Pick the right room shape

Reach InShallow closet, one open wall.
Square / RectangleStandard four-wall room.
L-ShapeOne corner returns. Two runs of wall.
U-ShapeWalls on three sides. Walk-in.
CustomAnything else. Shape it to fit the room.

Things to remember

✓Continue completes the current step and unlocks the next.
✓Back always works. You can revisit any step.
✓Changing the room shape restarts the room, so choose the closest shape up front.
✓You must plot at Systems before the Catalog opens.
✓Every room keeps its own progress and resumes where you left off.
✓A home with two rooms is tracked separately, per room.
✓We Design the closet in the app. We do not "draw" it.
