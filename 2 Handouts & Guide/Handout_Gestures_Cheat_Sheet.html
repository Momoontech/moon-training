Sales Designer App · Cheat Sheet

Gestures & Hands-On Moves

The taps and drags that make the app feel easy. Practice these first.
On the iPad
Floor Plan

Reshape a wall

  1. Tap a corner point on the plan.
  2. Hold, then drag it to the new spot.
  3. Release. The wall follows the corner.
Why: matches the plan to the real room measurements.
Floor Plan

Add a new wall

  1. Hold and drag on an existing wall.
  2. Pull the new wall out to length.
  3. Release to drop it in place.
Why: build L, U, and custom shapes from a simple wall.
Floor Plan

Drop an obstacle

  1. Pick a door, window, or outlet.
  2. Drag it toward a wall.
  3. It snaps to the wall automatically.
Why: the design accounts for the real room, no floating obstacles.
Measure & Design

Plot the design

  1. Start from the wall you picked.
  2. Add sections from the Catalog.
  3. Plot at Systems to open the Catalog.
Why: sections build onto the wall, not a blank page.
Present

Customize & Accessorize

  1. Customize changes finishes and colors.
  2. Accessorize adds the extras and add-ons.
  3. Review it live before pricing.
Why: this is where the closet becomes the client's.
Estimate

Price it live

  1. Toggle an item off and the total updates instantly.
  2. Show price, savings, and payment.
  3. Generate the quote and documents.
Why: the client sees the impact of each choice in real time.
